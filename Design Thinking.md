# Design Thinking Process

**Short Introduction**

Design Thinking is a structured, human-centered approach to problem-solving that emphasizes understanding user needs, reframing challenges, and iteratively developing solutions. It was employed in the development of AgriConnect to ensure the platform directly addresses the real-world difficulties faced by agricultural stakeholders—farmers seeking market access, buyers looking for reliable produce sources, delivery personnel needing efficient order fulfillment, and administrators managing platform operations. By applying the five stages of Empathize, Define, Ideation, Prototype, and Testing, the development team was able to translate contextual pain points into a functional digital marketplace that supports end-to-end agricultural transactions.
